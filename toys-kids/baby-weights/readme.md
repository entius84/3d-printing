# Baby Weights (Infant Dumbbell Toy)

An ergonomic, child-safe toy dumbbell designed for infants and toddlers, featuring a comfortable grip handle and fully rounded weight plates.

## Overview
Originally developed as a lightweight, engaging motor-skills toy for a 9-month-old, this model provides a fun, tactile prop for early coordination play without any sharp edges or small detachable components. Its monolithic single-piece geometry ensures there are zero choking hazards or loose parts.

## Print Parameters & Settings
* **Material:** Clean, non-toxic PLA or PETG.
* **Infill:** 15% to 25% infill.
* **Perimeters/Walls:** Recommended 3 to 4 wall loops to ensure high structural rigidity and prevent cracking under heavy toddler handling.
* **Supports:** None required. Print flat along the build plate for optimal layer adhesion across the handle-to-weight joints.
