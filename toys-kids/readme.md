## 🌟 Featured Projects

### Baby Weights (`/toys-kids/baby-weights/`)
* **Description:** A custom-designed, lightweight dumbbell toy featuring an ergonomic handle and rounded weight plates tailored for small hands.
* **Safety & Print Guidelines:** 
  * **Material:** Print in clean, non-toxic PLA or PETG.
  * **Settings:** Increase wall loops (at least 3-4 perimeters) rather than relying solely on high infill to ensure solid structural integrity so parts cannot crack or snap under heavy toddler use.
  * **Design Note:** Monolithic single-piece design to eliminate small detachable parts or choking hazards.
